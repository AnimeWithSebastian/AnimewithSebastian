Calling Jaadugar "underrated" is still not giving it enough.

Jaadugar: A Witch in Mongolia is on Crunchyroll right now. And every video is calling it a hidden gem or the best anime nobody is watching. That framing is too soft for what this show actually is.

Sitara is a young girl living in 13th-century Persia. She is a slave. She studies medicine and mathematics under a scholar named Fatima. She reads. She learns. She builds a life inside a world that has no room for her.

Then the Mongol Empire arrives. Her city burns. Everything she built is gone in episode two.

Here is the wrong take everyone keeps making: this is not an anime about magic. Jaadugar means magician. But Sitara's power is not a spell. It is knowledge. The title is a misdirection. She survives because she is the smartest person in every room — and nobody around her knows it yet.

The studio that made this is Science SARU. Same studio behind Dandadan. The animation is completely different — slower, more deliberate, built for weight instead of speed. That choice is the point. Every frame earns what happens next.

Sitara does not get a power-up. She does not discover she is special. She survives by knowing things that other people do not value yet.

That is a different kind of anime. And the "underrated" crowd is still underselling it — drop it.

Suzuki got quieter. Whether that is growth or the show losing its edge — that is the whole argument of Season 2. Drop it.
