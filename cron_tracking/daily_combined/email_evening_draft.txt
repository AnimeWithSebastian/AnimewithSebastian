Kagurabachi's Kunishige had a blade at his throat and didn't even look away from the fire. He's mid-forge at the Great Forge, and the Masumi attackers aren't just trying to kill him — they're trying to stop the smelting process itself, because interfering with the furnace or the ventilation system halts the enchanted blade completely, and if that process stops here, the report says everything eventually fails for good. Their leader slips past every defense and gets a blade against Kunishige's neck, unable to understand why he won't even flinch. Kunishige just keeps forging, thinking only of Chiaki — the person he finally has a real chance to protect, after years of believing he failed everyone close to him. He's not fighting alone, either — Subaru and Joji are already holding off the Masumi themselves, both running on zero sleep. Then Shiba, already critically wounded from an earlier fight and barely standing, teleports in and rips the attacker off him, refusing to back down even with his own stomach cut open and his life on the line. It echoes Mashiro's resolve from earlier, just as badly hurt and still refusing to stop. More Masumi agents keep coming, and Shiba keeps teleporting them away, one after another, telling Kunishige plainly that this time, he has to be the one to take center stage. Kunishige never once looks up from the flame. What does it take to earn protection like that? Leave your take.
