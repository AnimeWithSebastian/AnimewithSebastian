━━━ VO (235 words — fills the resolved 68s CapCut edit) ━━━
Tanya's own commander just used her fame as a weapon against her. Hans, the one officer treating her like a person, gets reassigned to the Eastern Front after pushing leadership to reject the war entirely. They refuse. Kurt tells Hans they could still win — but he doesn't sound like he believes it. The Empire is already stretched thin across multiple fronts, and Tanya realizes the government has no intention of ending anything. Then Hans calls her back — not to protect her, but to use her. Leadership sees her as one of their biggest threats, so instead of hiding that, he weaponizes it: he sends her to defend an abandoned railway depot with no retreat route, betting the entire Eastern Front on the enemy walking into a trap built around her name alone. Tanya calls it closer to a sacrifice than a mission — a suicide assignment wearing a promotion's paperwork. Then it gets worse. A spy inside the Empire hands the Allied Kingdom exactly what they need: her position, her movements, the cracks between her command and the officers above her. Reports even suggest the Federation is watching, waiting for the moment she's exposed. The mission ends with her second-in-command, Weiss, seriously wounded in the final battlefield sequence — the episode won't say if he makes it. What would you do with a commander who protects your reputation by risking your life? Leave your take.
