## VO draft v2 (trimmed to fit 45s target ~148-162 words)

Tanya's own commander just used her fame as a weapon against her. Hans, the one officer treating her like a person, gets reassigned to the Eastern Front after pushing leadership to end the war. They refuse. Tanya loses the one ally who might've pulled her out of combat for good. Then Hans calls her back — not to protect her, but to use her. Leadership sees her as one of their biggest threats, so instead of hiding that, he weaponizes it: he sends her to defend an abandoned railway depot with no retreat route, betting the Eastern Front on the enemy walking into a trap built around her name. Tanya calls it a suicide mission wearing a promotion's paperwork. A spy inside the Empire then hands the Allied Kingdom exactly what they need to know she's there. The mission ends with her second-in-command, Weiss, seriously wounded — the episode won't say if he makes it. What would you do with a commander who protects your reputation by risking your life?
