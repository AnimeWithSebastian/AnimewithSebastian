Kengo Kuga does not lose the riverboat chase in Sparks of Tomorrow Episode 7—he changes teams on it. The episode opens with a betrothal ceremony for Yosuke Mizoe and fifteen-year-old Inako, but Inako's older sister takes her place as the decoy. That lets Inako and Sasaki run for the neighboring community, where Kate's father supposedly has the other half of the electrical catalogue. Yosuke follows, and Kengo joins the pursuit, boarding their riverboat close enough to end the escape himself. Sasaki then reminds Kengo of Seiroku, Sasaki's brother and Kengo's war buddy. Kengo chooses to help the pair, turning a pursuit into an alliance while Yosuke is still trying to close the gap. The move is more than a rescue beat: Kengo has to decide whether Seiroku's memory means obeying the chase or protecting the people Sasaki is risking everything for. That is why the scene lands as a character decision, not a convenient interruption. That shift also changes what the boat represents. It starts as Yosuke's closing trap, then becomes the place where Kengo refuses to let the pursuit decide Inako's future. His choice makes the old war connection matter in the present. Did Seiroku earn Kengo's turn, or did the episode rush it? Leave your take. Spoilers aside, Kengo's choice gives this escape a new direction.
