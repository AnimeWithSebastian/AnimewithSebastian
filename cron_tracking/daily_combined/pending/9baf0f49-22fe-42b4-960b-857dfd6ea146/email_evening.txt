Grand Blue Season 3 turns Kohei's wingman plan into the funniest evidence against him in Episode 6. Kohei realizes Aina likes Iori and starts trying to steer Iori toward her instead of saying the obvious part out loud. His hints are so transparent that the whole situation grows stranger instead of clearer. Chisa and Aina get their own wrong read and try to give the boys space. Then Maki overhears enough to conclude that Iori and Kohei are romantically involved. That is the turn: Kohei begins as the guy trying to create one relationship, then accidentally becomes the proof of a completely different one. The joke works because no one is reacting to the same conversation; each person is building a new version of it from fragments. It also gives Aina and Chisa an open conversation about their feelings beneath the chaos. Kohei is never lying about what he wants, which makes the mistake even better: every attempt to make the plan clearer gives someone else a stranger version to believe. By the time Maki connects the dots, the audience can see why the conclusion is wrong and why it still fits every hint. Did Maki's conclusion make Kohei a worse wingman, or the episode's best punchline? Leave your take. Grand Blue gets the laugh by letting the misunderstanding do all the work.
