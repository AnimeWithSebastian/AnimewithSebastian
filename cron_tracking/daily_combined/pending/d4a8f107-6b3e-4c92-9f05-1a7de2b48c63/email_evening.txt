━━━ VO (215 words — fills the resolved 60s CapCut edit) ━━━
Three Gotei Thirteen captains hit Gerard Valkyrie with everything they had this week, and the only people bleeding when it ended were them. Spoiler warning for Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War, The Calamity, episode three, which aired August eighth. Toshiro Hitsugaya opens by freezing Gerard's hand solid. Kenpachi Zaraki follows with a classic Kenpachi entrance and cuts that hand clean off. It grows back immediately, which is the episode telling you exactly what kind of problem this is. Byakuya Kuchiki adds a swarm of cherry blossoms from Senbonzakura Kageyoshi. Three captains, three completely different answers, and Gerard leaves the exchange mostly uninjured. Then the episode reframes the entire fight. When the captains damage his sword Hoffnung, the wounds do not stay on Gerard. They land on the captains instead. The Miracle does not simply heal him, it hands the damage back to whoever dealt it. That is not a power you can out-damage, and three of the Gotei Thirteen's heaviest hitters just spent an entire episode proving it. The episode ends there, unresolved, all three still standing in front of him, with the climax held for episode four. So is there any amount of raw force that actually solves Gerard, or does the answer have to come from somewhere else entirely? Leave your take.
