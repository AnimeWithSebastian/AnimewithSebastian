## VO draft v3 (CORRECTED after source re-check — "coughing blood" was NOT confirmed by any fetched source and has been removed; "So what?" corrected to the actual quoted line)

SOURCE CORRECTION LOG: AOL's recap explicitly does NOT state Shiba is coughing blood, and does not give his exact dialogue. A second source, Syvduck's chapter-128 breakdown video (youtube.com/watch?v=yqAGZyIPPbo), supplies the real quote and confirms Shiba is described as being at critically low health from prior wounds reopening — not coughing blood on-panel. VO rewritten to only claim what's actually sourced.

Kagurabachi's Kunishige had a blade at his throat and didn't even look away from the fire. He's a swordsmith mid-forge, racing to finish an enchanted blade before the enemy Masumi can stop him. Their leader slips past everyone and gets a blade to his neck — and Kunishige just keeps forging, thinking only of Chiaki, the person he's already failed to protect once before. Then Shiba, already critically wounded from an earlier fight, teleports in and rips the attacker off him, saying flatly: "So what if I do? Even if you kill me, we're not giving up this fight." Kunishige never once looks up from the flame. What does it take to earn protection like that? Leave your take.
