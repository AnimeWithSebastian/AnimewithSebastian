### STEP 4 — ANGLE GENERATION RESEARCH STACK

SEARCH 1 — TRENDING ANIME (what is the anime community talking about right now)
Query: "anime trending Reddit YouTube [current month year]"
Goal: Identify 3-5 shows with active discussion. Note traction tier for each.

SEARCH 2 — SHOW-SPECIFIC TRACTION
Query: "[selected show] anime Reddit discussion [current month year]"
Goal: Confirm show has active fandom discussion. Assign traction tier.
Traction Tiers:
  T1_AIRING — currently airing, episode-by-episode discussion
  T2_CONTROVERSY — finished/upcoming but high active debate
  T3_NOSTALGIA — anniversary, rewatch, or throwback wave
  T4_EVERGREEN — stable consistent traffic, no peak event

SEARCH 3 — FANDOM GAP
Query: "[show] [character/moment] overlooked detail hidden fact Reddit"
Goal: Find the angle the fandom has not fully addressed.
Gap Categories:
  GAP_1_UNFINISHED_IMPLICATION — show established something, fandom never followed it through
  GAP_2_MISSING_CONTEXT — viewers lack a key piece of information that reframes everything
  GAP_3_RECONTEXTUALIZATION — known fact that means something different with new framing
  GAP_4_ORIGIN_STORY — real-world or in-universe origin nobody has connected

SEARCH 4 — CONSENSUS CHECK
Query: "[show] [angle topic] Reddit popular opinion"
Goal: Confirm the angle avoids fandom consensus. If consensus already covers it — find new angle.

COMPETITOR DATA RULE — HARD LAW (user instruction June 12, 2026):
Competitor research is for SIGNAL ONLY. Never for recreation.
The question is never "what did they make" — it is "what does their performance tell us"
about what format, topic, or hook structure the audience responds to right now.

WHAT SIGNAL MEANS:
  FORMAT SIGNAL: If LIST performs at 10M+ on a competitor → LIST is a viable format this week.
  TOPIC SIGNAL: If One Piece is getting massive views → One Piece has active audience.
  HOOK SIGNAL: If curiosity-gap titles are outperforming statement titles → use that structure.

WHAT SIGNAL DOES NOT MEAN:
  Do NOT recreate their video.
  Do NOT use their angle.
  Do NOT use their specific question or framing.
  Do NOT make the same show + same format + same hook.
  Their video already exists. The audience already saw it.
  Making the same thing is not competition — it is redundancy.

ORIGINALITY IS NON-NEGOTIABLE (user instruction):
  AnimeWithSebastian makes original content. Always.
  Take the format signal. Build a completely different angle.
  Take the topic signal. Find the gap the competitor didn’t go near.
  Take the hook structure. Apply it to a different show, character, or claim.

EXAMPLE — RIGHT:
  Signal: Orewashimo's "Don't watch these anime with your parents" (7.6M) → curiosity-gap LIST is working.
  Application: Original curiosity-gap LIST on a completely different angle — different show, different claim.

EXAMPLE — WRONG:
  "Don't watch these anime with your parents" already exists at 7.6M.
  Making the same video = zero original value. HARD STOP.
