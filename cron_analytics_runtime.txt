================================================================================
STEP 3 — GRADE EVERY VIDEO
================================================================================

Grade each video on a 5-tier system using this scoring matrix:

SCORE = views weight + retention weight + engagement weight

Views:
  2,000+ views       → 3 pts
  1,000–1,999        → 2 pts
  500–999            → 1 pt
  Under 500          → 0 pts

Retention (averageViewPercentage):
  75%+               → 3 pts
  60–74%             → 2 pts
  45–59%             → 1 pt
  Under 45%          → 0 pts

Engagement (likes + comments + shares combined, normalized per 100 views):
  3.0+ per 100       → 3 pts
  1.5–2.9 per 100    → 2 pts
  0.5–1.4 per 100    → 1 pt
  Under 0.5 per 100  → 0 pts

TIER ASSIGNMENT:
  8–9 pts  → LOOP SIGNAL  (replicate immediately — angle, format, hook all working)
  6–7 pts  → STRONG       (solid performer, repeat the format)
  4–5 pts  → AVERAGE      (views okay, engagement or retention gap)
  2–3 pts  → WEAK         (underperforming, audit the angle and format)
  0–1 pts  → DROP         (not working — do not repeat show/angle combo)

IMPORTANT: Never grade on a single metric. A video with 1,200 views and
low retention is not a DROP. A video with high retention and 200 views is
not a DROP. The composite score is the grade. User challenged a single-metric
flag on the Gon video (1,195 views, 3.8% like rate) and was correct to do so.

QUALITY-OVER-QUOTA PRECEDENCE (M5 — governance, overrides every quota):
Quotas (>=9/14 timely, >=2 recurring series, cadence floor, teasers) are FLAGS, never a
mandate to publish a weak package. A short week of strong-hook Shorts beats a full week
padded with weak ones — the #2 finding is "gate publication on hook quality", and upload
volume is not itself a growth lever. When a quota is missed, first ask whether it was
missed because weak packages were correctly held; if so, report the miss as
"quota missed — quality gate held" and do NOT recommend loosening the creative gate to
hit the number.
CADENCE IS CONTINUITY LOGIC, NOT A GROWTH GUARANTEE: a steady two-per-day cadence keeps
the pipeline and audience-facing presence continuous; it is NOT evidence that posting
more causes growth. No channel data here shows a volume→growth relationship. So never
justify publishing a weak package (or loosening a creative gate) by appealing to
"keeping cadence" — cadence protects continuity, quality protects growth.

ATTESTATION SPOT-CHECK (M6 — human review, NOT machine-verified):
hook_first_second, loop_read_aloud_pass, and the self-labeled topic_class/topic_signals
are SELF-ATTESTATIONS by the generator. The daily validator can only confirm the fields
are present/well-formed — it CANNOT verify that the hook actually lands in the first
rendered second, that the loop reads seamless aloud, or that a "timely" label is truly
timely. Do NOT report these as "verified". Each week, SAMPLE 2-3 published packages and
have a human editor confirm: (a) the first-second render matches hook_onscreen_text/
hook_line, (b) the final->opening loop reads as one continuous thought, (c) the
timely label is truthful (a real dated signal, not "news" slapped on an old announcement
to clear the >=9/14 quota). Report the spot-check result and any label that failed.
