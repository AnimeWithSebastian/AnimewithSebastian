## THE LAW

Every email script package must include separate titles and hashtags for YouTube and TikTok. One set does not cover both. Each set must follow that platform's rules.

---

## YOUTUBE RULES

**Title:**
- Max 100 characters
- Front-load the hook — most important words in first 60 characters (mobile truncates after ~60)
- No clickbait that misrepresents the video
- Can use punctuation, dashes, brackets for structure
- Capitalization: Title Case or sentence case — no ALL CAPS titles
- No hashtags in the YouTube title

**Hashtags:**
- Place in description, not title
- Max 3 hashtags recommended (YouTube auto-surfaces topics; more than 3 dilutes)
- Format: #HashtagNoSpaces
- Use broad + specific combo: one broad (#anime), one show-specific (#YuYuHakusho), one content-type (#animefacts or #animeshorts)
- YouTube ignores hashtags beyond 15 — never exceed 5

---

## TIKTOK RULES

**Title/Caption:**
- Max 2,200 characters but algorithm favors under 150 characters
- Hooks work better as questions or bold statements
- Hashtags go IN the caption — not separate
- Can be more casual/conversational than YouTube title
- Emojis allowed and perform well on TikTok

**Hashtags:**
- 3–5 hashtags in the caption itself
- Mix: 1 mega tag (#anime — billions of views), 1–2 mid tags (#animefacts, #animeshorts), 1 niche tag (show-specific)
- Avoid over-tagging — TikTok penalizes hashtag stuffing
- Format: #HashtagNoSpaces directly in caption

---

## REQUIRED FORMAT IN EVERY EMAIL PACKAGE

```
YOUTUBE
Title: [max 100 chars, front-loaded hook, Title Case]
Hashtags (in description): #Tag1 #Tag2 #Tag3

TIKTOK
Caption: [under 150 chars, casual, hook-first, hashtags included]
```

---

## VIOLATION CONDITIONS

- Sending one generic title for both platforms = LAW VIOLATION
- Sending hashtags without platform label = LAW VIOLATION
- YouTube title over 100 characters = LAW VIOLATION
- TikTok caption over 150 characters = LAW VIOLATION (flag if approaching)
- More than 5 hashtags on YouTube = LAW VIOLATION
- More than 5 hashtags on TikTok = LAW VIOLATION
