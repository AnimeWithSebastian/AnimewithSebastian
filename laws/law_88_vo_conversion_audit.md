# Law #88 — VO Conversion Audit (5 Triggers)

**Added:** June 29, 2026
**Sources:**
- YouTubeNiches.com — June 4, 2026 (comments = #1 algorithm signal; personality/voice drives subscriber conversion)
- viralhookanalyzer.com — June 8, 2026 (5,000 Shorts study)
**Enforced in:** STEP 10 VO Quality Check (VO Conversion Audit block) + EMAIL ASSEMBLY block 8B

## Rule

Every VO must be checked against 5 Conversion Triggers before it is sent.
Minimum 2 of 5 triggers required. Target: 3 or more.
If fewer than 2 triggers present = REVISE. Do not send.

The VO Conversion Audit block MUST appear as a separate labeled block (8B) in the email.
Missing block = CANNOT SEND.

## Research Basis

Comments are the #1 algorithm signal on YouTube — stronger than likes.
Personality and voice drive subscriber conversion. Viewers follow people, not videos.
A VO that does not signal personality, does not invite debate, and does not leave something unresolved will get views but not convert to subscribers. The conversion gap on AnimeWithSebastian (0.043% actual vs 0.1% target as of June 28 analytics) is closing line and hook tension — not reach.

## The 5 Conversion Triggers

### TRIGGER 1 — Re-Watch Confession
**Most powerful conversion signal.**
A line that implies this video rewards a second watch. Signals obsessive viewing.
Viewer thinks: "I need to go back and watch this again now."
Check: Does any line imply there is more to find on rewatch?

### TRIGGER 2 — Specific Feeling Named
Names what the viewer already felt about this show/moment, but could not articulate.
NOT a label ("sad", "epic", "amazing").
A TEXTURE — something specific ("the kind of quiet that means something is wrong", "that moment where you realize you were rooting for the wrong person").
Check: Does the VO name a specific feeling the viewer recognizes?

### TRIGGER 3 — Community Signal
A CTA tied to THIS video's specific emotional register — not a generic prompt.
**NOT ALLOWED:** "Like and comment" / "What do you think?" — too generic, zero conversion value.
**ALLOWED:** "Who else rewatched this?" / "Am I wrong about this?" / "What did you think the first time?"
Check: Is the CTA specific to the angle and feeling of this VO?

### TRIGGER 4 — Open Question Closer
The final line leaves something unresolved.
The viewer MUST comment to resolve it. Not a rhetorical question — a real one with two possible answers.
The closer should create two sides. If it closes cleanly, the viewer has no reason to comment.
Check: Does the last line close with an open question or a genuinely debatable claim?

### TRIGGER 5 — Personality Signature
One line that could NOT be copy-pasted by any other channel.
Sebastian's voice only. His specific framing of the world, the show, the moment.
If you removed the channel name and dropped this VO onto any other anime channel — it still sounds like Sebastian.
Check: Is there one line in this VO that could only come from this creator?

## Email Format — Block 8B

```
─── VO CONVERSION AUDIT ───
TRIGGER 1 (Re-Watch Confession): YES / NO
TRIGGER 2 (Specific Feeling Named): YES / NO
TRIGGER 3 (Community Signal): YES / NO
TRIGGER 4 (Open Question Closer): YES / NO
TRIGGER 5 (Personality Signature): YES / NO
TRIGGERS HIT: X/5 | STATUS: PASS / REVISE
─────────────────────────
```

PASS = 2 or more triggers hit.
REVISE = 0 or 1 trigger. Rewrite the VO before sending.
