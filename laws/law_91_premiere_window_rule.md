# Law #91 — Premiere Window Rule

**Added:** June 29, 2026
**Source:** Anime Corner Summer 2026 anticipation rankings June 27 2026 | OtakuKart Summer 2026 lineup June 24 2026
**Applies to:** Every video recommendation, every cron slot

## Rule

Any show with a confirmed premiere within 14 days = PRIORITY TIER.

Pre-premiere content owns the discovery window. Post-premiere content competes with every other anime channel covering the same show after episode 1 drops.

## Priority Tiers

**PREMIERE WINDOW (0–14 days before air):**
- Audience is actively searching, not yet saturated with reactions
- Competitor gap is maximum — most channels wait for the episode
- Discovery traffic highest during this window
- Required angle: "why you need to watch this" / "what makes this different" / "the story behind this show"

**PREMIERE WEEK (0–7 days after air):**
- Reaction and first-impression content works here
- Competition spikes — channel needs a specific angle, not generic review
- Still viable if the angle is unique

**POST-PREMIERE (7+ days after air):**
- Requires a clear competitor gap or a character/theme angle no one else has covered
- Default pass unless research shows the angle is open
