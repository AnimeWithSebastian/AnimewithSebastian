# Law #92 — Fan Voice Standard

**Added:** June 29, 2026
**Source:** Channel analytics audit June 29 2026 — VO voice identified as root cause of weak sub conversion and low comment rate
**Applies to:** Every VO — both runtime slots, every manual package

## Rule

Every VO must pass the Fan Voice Check before it is sent.

## The Fan Voice Check

Read the VO out loud. Then ask: **Would a real anime fan say this to a friend, or does it sound like an essay?**

If it sounds like an essay = FAIL. Rewrite the angle first.

## BANNED Voice Patterns

These make the VO sound like it was written by AI explaining anime to a stranger:

- Formal sentence structures: "the thing that makes X unbeatable is Y"
- Academic framing: "not as a tactic. as a reflex."
- Thesis signaling: "that is what happens when..."
- Over-explaining the obvious: "every monster in that world fights on instinct — territory, survival, power"
- Restating the setup before the point
- Any sentence that sounds like a Wikipedia intro paragraph

## REQUIRED Voice Patterns

These sound like a fan who actually watched the show and can't stop thinking about it:

- Short punchy sentences that land one hit at a time
- At least one line that uses the specific language fans actually use ("he just didn't lose", "that scene destroyed everyone")
- Reactions embedded in the analysis ("and nobody talks about this")
- Stakes felt, not explained ("his mom didn't leave because she wanted to — she left because he kept becoming someone she didn't recognize")
- The closer must feel like something you'd actually say watching with a friend

## Relationship to Other Laws

Fan Voice Check is CHECK 9 in STEP 10 — added after Law #88 Conversion Audit.
A VO can pass all 8 existing checks and still fail Law #92 if it reads like an essay.
Law #92 failure = CANNOT SEND regardless of other scores.

## Why This Law Exists

Channel audience is 52.6% male 18–34 and 21.3% male 13–17. Both demographics have zero tolerance for essay-voice content. They watch because it sounds like someone who actually loves the show. The moment it sounds like analysis for its own sake, they swipe.
