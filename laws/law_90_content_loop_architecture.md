# Law #90 — Content Loop Architecture

**Added:** June 29, 2026
**Source:** YouTube subscriber conversion research (June 2026) + channel analytics audit
**Applies to:** Every cron slot, every manual package

## Rule

Every 3 consecutive videos sent must form a LOOP — not 3 random videos on 3 random shows.

The loop has three video types:

- **Video 1 — Discovery Hook:** Earns attention from someone who has never seen the channel. High hook tension. Broad appeal within the niche. Premieres, trending shows, viral moments.
- **Video 2 — Trust Build:** Goes deeper on the same show, same character type, or same analytical angle as Video 1. Someone who watched Video 1 gets rewarded for coming back.
- **Video 3 — Identity Signature:** Makes the viewer feel this channel has a distinct voice and point of view they cannot get elsewhere. Creates the "I need to follow this" moment.

## Enforcement

- After every 3 videos sent, check: do they form a loop?
- If 3 consecutive videos are on 3 unrelated shows with no connecting thread = LOOP BROKEN
- Broken loop = next video MUST reconnect to a show or angle already covered
- One connecting thread can be: same show, same analytical lens (power cost, identity, betrayal), same format type

## Why This Law Exists

Channel analytics (June 29 2026): top video has 4,943 views and only 0 net subscribers. The JJK video has 2,611 views and 4 subscribers (0.153% conversion — best on the channel). Viewers are watching and not subscribing because there is no pattern to follow. A loop creates the pattern. Subscribe happens on video 3, not video 1.
