# LAW #72 — LONG-FORM PRE-PUBLISH CHECKLIST

**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Channel:** AnimeWithSebastian (@animewithsebastian)  
**Applies To:** Long-form videos ONLY  
**Short-Form Equivalent:** Law #58 (Pre-Send Verification — the enforcement gate for Shorts)  
**Date Added:** 2026-06-12

---

## CORE RULE

No long-form video publishes without passing all six gates.

This checklist is not optional. It is not a suggestion.  
It runs before every long-form — the same way Law #58 runs before every Short gets sent.

If any gate fails — the video does not publish until it is fixed.

---

## THE SIX GATES

---

### GATE 1 — VOICE CHECK (Law #67)

**Question:** Does this video sound like a real person talking about anime — or does it sound like a script being performed?

**Pass criteria:**
- [ ] No formal transitions ("Moving on," "As we can see," "In conclusion")
- [ ] No robotic phrasing or AI-sounding delivery patterns
- [ ] The first 30 seconds sounds like the middle of a conversation, not an intro
- [ ] The closing section sounds like a person landing an idea — not wrapping up a report
- [ ] CTA is casual and direct — does not sound like a promo

**If failed:** Rewrite the flagged sections before recording. Do not record over a script that fails the voice check.

---

### GATE 2 — COMMENT ENGINEERING CHECK (Law #68)

**Question:** Does this video give the audience something specific to react to — a take, a comparison, a question they actually want to answer?

**Pass criteria:**
- [ ] At least one moment in Section 3 (Argument) or Section 4 (Open Closer) is designed to generate a response
- [ ] The comment prompt is NOT a generic "What do you think?" — it is tied to the specific argument of the video
- [ ] The closing question or statement is delivered naturally — it does not feel like a planted prompt
- [ ] The video does not end flat — there is a hook for the comment section

**If failed:** Add or rework the Open Closer (Section 4). The comment hook lives there.

---

### GATE 3 — FACT VERIFICATION CHECK (Law #69)

**Question:** Is every specific claim in this video verified against a live source — not memory, not assumption?

**Pass criteria:**
- [ ] Every character stat, power system rule, and timeline claim is sourced to manga, anime episode, or verified wiki
- [ ] Every creator comparison (view counts, upload frequency, format patterns) is sourced to live data from the actual channel
- [ ] Every claim that uses specific numbers ("X times stronger," "Y seconds," "Z episodes") has a source confirmed before recording
- [ ] No claim is included because "it sounds right" — if the source cannot be found, the claim is cut

**Source log (fill before recording):**

```
FACT LOG — [Video Title]
Claim: [exact claim text]
Source: [URL or episode reference]
Verified: YES / NO

Claim: [exact claim text]
Source: [URL or episode reference]
Verified: YES / NO
```

**If failed:** Do not record. Verify the facts or cut the claims. Unverified claims are not published under any circumstances.

---

### GATE 4 — DANGLING CLAIMS CHECK (Law #70)

**Question:** Does every claim opened in this video get resolved before the CTA?

**Pass criteria:**
- [ ] All open claims are listed in the Claim Log
- [ ] Every claim in the Claim Log has an assigned close section (1–4)
- [ ] No claim reaches Section 5 (CTA) unresolved
- [ ] If a Part 2 hook exists — the current video still delivers a complete, satisfying thought

**Claim log (fill before recording):**

```
CLAIM LOG — [Video Title]
1. [Claim text] → Closes in Section ___
2. [Claim text] → Closes in Section ___
3. [Claim text] → Closes in Section ___
```

**If failed:** Restructure the script. Close every loop before Section 5 or cut the unresolvable claim entirely.

---

### GATE 5 — STRUCTURE CHECK (Law #71)

**Question:** Does this video follow the 5-section structure without falling into essay or lecture mode?

**Pass criteria:**
- [ ] Video uses the 5-section structure: Hook → Setup → Argument → Open Closer → CTA
- [ ] No section uses academic/essay framing (thesis, evidence, conclusion)
- [ ] No banned transitions appear in the script (see Law #71 banned list)
- [ ] Argument section develops one idea — does not list numbered points
- [ ] Pacing check passed:

| Section | Target Length | Passes? |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 15–30 sec | [ ] |
| Setup | 60–90 sec | [ ] |
| Argument | 3–5 min | [ ] |
| Open Closer | 60–90 sec | [ ] |
| CTA | 15–20 sec | [ ] |

**If failed:** Revise the section that failed pacing or structure before recording.

---

### GATE 6 — ORIGINALITY CHECK (Law #65 applied to long-form)

**Question:** Is this video original — a genuine take from AnimeWithSebastian — or is it recreating something that already exists?

**Pass criteria:**
- [ ] If competitor content was reviewed as research, it was used as SIGNAL ONLY — the angle, framing, and voice are original
- [ ] The video does not retell a story that a viewer could find on any other channel — it adds a specific take or angle
- [ ] The channel name and content are independent — no assumption that the viewer knows the channel's branding (Law #64 applied to long-form)
- [ ] The video does not AI-voice the VO — the creator's voice is used (Law #67)

**If failed:** The video is not ready. Rework the angle before recording.

---

## WHEN THIS CHECKLIST RUNS

- Before every long-form script is finalized
- Before every long-form is recorded
- Before every long-form is uploaded

It is not an afterthought. It is not run after the video is done.  
The earlier a gate fails, the less work is wasted.

Run Gate 3 (Fact Verification) and Gate 4 (Dangling Claims) at the script stage.  
Run Gate 1 (Voice), Gate 5 (Structure), and Gate 6 (Originality) before recording.  
Run Gate 2 (Comment Engineering) before uploading — confirm the Open Closer still works after editing.

---

## WHY THIS EXISTS

Law #58 exists because Shorts were going out with unverified claims.  
Law #72 exists because long-form has more surface area for the same problems — and the consequences are larger.

A Short with a bad claim is 60 seconds of damage.  
A long-form with a bad claim is 8 minutes of damage — and it lives on the channel permanently.

The checklist is the last line of defense.  
It is not optional.
