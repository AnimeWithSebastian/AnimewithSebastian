## THE CORE FINDING

Channel has strong views and retention. The gap is comments.
27 comments across 35 videos = 0.77 per video.
Target: 3 per video → 7 per video over 90 days.

The comment section does not fill itself. It has to be engineered.
Every script must activate at least 3 of the 5 community levers below.

## THE 5 COMMUNITY LEVERS

Activate minimum 3 per package. If fewer than 3 are active — revise before send.

LEVER 1 — THE OPEN POSITION
The VO states the creator's actual take clearly enough that a viewer
can agree or disagree with it in one sentence.
A neutral take gets no comment. A stated position gets a response.

LEVER 2 — THE DEBATE SPLIT
The angle creates two clear sides. Not a question — a divide.
Viewer either thinks you're right or you're wrong.
Both outcomes produce a comment.
Test: Can you name both sides in one line?
"Team Erwin / Team Armin" — that's a debate split.
"What did you think?" — that's not.

LEVER 3 — THE SPECIFIC DETAIL NOBODY MENTIONED
Drop one detail in the VO that a real fan would either know or not know.
This creates two comment types: "I knew this" (validation comment)
and "wait what, really?" (correction instinct comment).
Either one is a win.

LEVER 4 — THE UNFINISHED THOUGHT
The closer leaves one thread open — something the viewer needs to add
or argue to feel like the conversation is complete.
Not a question. A thought that doesn't fully resolve.
The viewer finishes it in the comments.

LEVER 5 — THE PINNED COMMENT POSITION
The pinned comment does two things:
1. States the creator's position directly
2. Leaves a specific open thread — not a generic question

Wrong: "What do you think about this scene?"
Right: "Erwin asked men to die for him his whole career. Armin never had to. That's the difference. who was actually right."

The pinned comment is the invitation. Make it specific.

## COMMUNITY ACTION — IN EVERY EMAIL

Every package email must include this block:

COMMUNITY ACTION REQUIRED:
Post time: [time]
Within 1 hour of posting — reply to the first strong comment you receive.
Don't just like it. Reply with your actual take.
One reply in the first hour doubles the comment count algorithmically.
If someone disagrees — engage it. That's the lever.
