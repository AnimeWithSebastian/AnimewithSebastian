## WHAT ORIGINS CONTENT IS

Shows and manga from the 70s through early 2000s that directly influenced
modern anime. The angle is always the connection — what modern fans love
traces back to something they've never heard of.

This is not nostalgia content. It is not "remember this classic?"
It is: "the thing you love was built from this."

Examples of correct Origins angles:
- Hiei (YYH, 1990) → Sasuke (Naruto, 1999) — Kishimoto modeled the brooding
  dark-powered loner on Hiei directly
- Guts (Berserk, 1989 manga) → the entire dark fantasy archetype
- Yusuke (YYH) → the delinquent protagonist that became Naruto's template
- Dragon Ball → the power scaling system every shounen borrowed
- Golgo 13 → the cold professional killer that every anime hitman comes from
