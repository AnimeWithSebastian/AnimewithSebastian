# Law #82 — No Intro Stamp / Brand Stamp (Videos Start on First Real Clip)

**Status:** ACTIVE — June 28, 2026
**Source:** FluxNote YouTube Shorts Success Checklist 2026 | User workflow confirmed June 28, 2026

## Rule
Every clip edit plan starts on CUT 1 = first real content clip with VO already running.

NO brand stamp. NO logo animation. NO intro sequence. NO channel graphic at the top.

## Why
- Intros on Shorts kill the first 1.5 seconds — the most critical window for retention
- YouTube Shorts algorithm measures swipe-away rate in first 2 seconds
- FluxNote 2026 data: "NO logo animation at start. First frame = hook already in progress."
- User's top loop videos (188.55%, 106.35% avg view %) achieved those numbers without an intro

## Enforcement
- CUT 1 in every clip plan = first content clip, no exceptions
- "Brand stamp" and "channel intro graphic" are permanently banned from clip plan language
- Applies to ALL formats, ALL traction tiers, morning / evening / manual sends
- Runtime total video runtime calculation does NOT include any intro time (none exists)

## Origin
User confirmed June 28, 2026: videos start directly on first real clip with VO.
Research confirmed: intro stamps hurt Shorts retention and swipe-away rate.
