## What This Law Does

Expands the rotation beyond premiere-window and character-dive packages.
Four new content types are approved for the cron system — each verified against monetization signals, comment-engagement data, and algorithm behavior.

## APPROVED ROTATION TYPES

### TYPE 1 — SEASON RATING (SR)
**What it is:** Rating/ranking the current anime season you are watching. Not a tier list essay — a short, opinionated verdict on 1 show per video.

**Why it works:**
- Tier list and ranking content drives the highest comment-rate of any anime format — viewers argue with your placement, correct you, and defend their own picks. Argument in comments = algorithm signal.
- Seasonal tier list videos average 3.8% engagement on YouTube Shorts per SociaVault 2026 — 1.4× higher than long-form baseline.
- Packapop 2026: "Rankings trigger comments ('you forgot X!') → more engagement."
- TikTok: debate-hook formats create watch + comment loops that extend session depth (Darkroom 2026).

**Rules:**
- One show per Short. Never rank 5 shows in one video — pick a side and defend it.
- State your rating in the HOOK (first 3 seconds). Don't build to it.
- Rating must be provocative enough to be wrong. Safe ratings get no comments.
- Must be a show currently airing OR just finished airing (within 30 days). No throwbacks in this format.
- Word count: 60–90w. Fan voice standard (Law #92) applies.

**Format code:** SEASON_RATING
**Slot eligibility:** Morning or Evening
**Brand-deal signal:** HIGH — demonstrates current-season authority, which is what Crunchyroll and streaming sponsors want to see.

---

### TYPE 2 — SEASON PREVIEW (SP)
**What it is:** Why you should watch or look forward to a new anime, new season, or new episode drop.

**Why it works:**
- Summer 2026 season preview content is actively being consumed right now — viewers are deciding what to watch.
- Premiere-window content has the best discovery ceiling because the algorithm is already surfacing the show name to new audiences.
- Series/episodic preview content: TikTok "often recommends episode 2 to viewers of episode 1" — preview content creates follow chains (Darkroom 2026).
- TTS Vibes 2026: educational series content hits 9.5–11.29% engagement for nano-influencers under 100K.

**Rules:**
- Must have a verified premiere date within 45 days (before OR after airing). Never preview something without a confirmed date.
- Angle must answer: WHY this show, for THIS viewer, right NOW. Not "here's the plot." The answer to "what am I signing up for emotionally?"
- Do NOT recap the trailer. Everyone has seen the trailer.
- Competitor check required: if 10+ channels already covered the exact angle, pivot the angle.
- Word count: 60–90w. Fan voice standard (Law #92) applies.

**Format code:** SEASON_PREVIEW
**Slot eligibility:** Morning or Evening
**Brand-deal signal:** HIGH — premiere-window content aligns directly with Crunchyroll/streaming sponsor calendar.

### TYPE 3 — MANGA VS ANIME (MVA)
**What it is:** A specific scene, arc, or moment where the manga and anime version are meaningfully different — and why it matters.

**Why it works:**
- FluxNote 2026: "The manga vs. anime comparison nobody noticed" is explicitly listed as a high-performing Shorts idea under the adaptation-differences category.
- JJK S3 manga vs anime comparison videos active on YouTube right now (March 2026), indicating live demand.
- This format creates two comment camps (manga readers vs anime-only viewers) — dual-audience debate drives comment rate above baseline.
- Saves/bookmarks are elevated on this format because viewers who haven't read the manga want to return and compare.
- Saves = strong algorithm signal on both TikTok and YouTube (TikTok algorithm 2026: saves are second only to shares).

**Rules:**
- Must be a SPECIFIC scene or moment — not "manga is better than anime" (general takes have no search ceiling).
- The difference must be meaningful: a cut scene, a changed emotional beat, a panel that hits differently, a scene the anime improved. No surface-level "the colors are different" content.
- You must take a side. Neutral comparisons don't generate comments.
- Must verify the difference via live source — NEVER from memory (Law #52).
- Word count: 60–90w. Fan voice standard (Law #92) applies.

**Format code:** MANGA_VS_ANIME
**Slot eligibility:** Morning or Evening
**Brand-deal signal:** MED-HIGH — manga publishers (Right Stuf, VIZ) specifically value creators who drive manga-adjacent conversations.

### TYPE 4 — EPISODE MOMENT (EM)
**What it is:** A single scene, episode beat, or new-enemy/new-character reveal from a currently-airing show — one moment, why it matters, what it means going forward.

**Why it works:**
- Episode reaction and breakdown content has the highest same-week search velocity of any anime format — people search "[Show] episode [N] explained" immediately after airing.
- Hook-and-reveal format (state the moment, then explain why it's bigger than it looks) achieves highest completion rates on both platforms (TikTok Algorithm 2026, Miraflow).
- New enemy or power-reveal content specifically: fan communities actively clip and repost this — shares are the highest-weighted signal on TikTok (Miraflow 2026).
- Comment rate 1%+ on Shorts flags discussion-worthy content for algorithm boost (Kompozy 2026).

**Rules:**
- Must air within 7 days of the episode dropping. After 7 days, the search window closes.
- One moment per video. Not "5 things from episode 8." Pick the single biggest beat.
- SPOILER WARNING must be in the title or first line of the caption. Spoiler content without warning creates negative feedback (swipe-away, report) — a suppression signal.
- Must be from a show with an active airing schedule. No reaction videos to episodes that aired 2+ seasons ago.
- New enemy reveals and power-reveals are the highest-value moments for this format — prioritize those over generic episode recaps.
- Word count: 60–90w. Fan voice standard (Law #92) applies.

**Format code:** EPISODE_MOMENT
**Slot eligibility:** Morning or Evening. For same-day episode drops, this type can be scheduled for the next morning's slot as a priority override.
**Brand-deal signal:** MED — high view velocity but shorter shelf life. Use to build subscriber conversion, not brand pitch portfolio.

## WHAT THESE FORMATS ARE NOT

- **NOT recap videos.** Do not summarize the plot. The fan already watched it. The angle is always: here's what you missed, here's what it means, here's what's wrong with the popular read, here's what to watch for.
- **NOT essay-voice.** All four types must pass the fan voice test from Law #92: read it out loud — does it sound like a friend who loves the show, or a Wikipedia article?
- **NOT general opinions.** "This season is mid" is not a SEASON_RATING. "Dandadan made the Turbo-Granny reveal hit harder in the anime than the manga because they gave her a sound design moment that doesn't exist on the page" is a MANGA_VS_ANIME.

---

## BLACKOUT RULES FOR NEW TYPES

- **SEASON_RATING:** 7-day blackout per show (same as standard). One rating per show per season — do not re-rate the same show in the same season.
- **SEASON_PREVIEW:** 7-day blackout from send date. 45-day premiere window rule enforced.
- **MANGA_VS_ANIME:** 14-day blackout per show (longer shelf life, no same-show repeat within two weeks).
- **EPISODE_MOMENT:** No blackout — episode-specific content is time-stamped and doesn't compete with itself. But the 7-day airing window is a hard deadline.

---

## MONETIZATION PRIORITY ORDER

When selecting format for an available slot:
1. T1 breaking news always wins (existing law)
2. EPISODE_MOMENT within 7-day window — highest same-week search velocity
3. SEASON_PREVIEW within premiere window — algorithm is already surfacing the show
4. SEASON_RATING for currently-airing shows — drives comment engagement and subscriber identity
5. MANGA_VS_ANIME — saves signal, dual-audience debate, bookmarkable content
