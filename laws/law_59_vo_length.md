# LAW #59 — VO LENGTH / NO ESSAY RULE
**System:** AnimeWithSebastian — v5.1
**Added:** June 2026
**Status:** ACTIVE

---

## THE LAW

The VO has no hard word cap. It has a structure cap.

The target is conversational length — not essay length.

---

## WHAT THIS MEANS

A VO that reads like a paper is not a Short. A VO that sounds like someone talking is.

The difference is not word count. The difference is structure.

---

## BANNED STRUCTURES (from v2.2 — REMOVED in v5.0)

These three patterns are banned. They signal essay mode, not conversation mode.

### 1. Essay Opener
**What it is:** Starting the VO with a thesis statement that announces what you are about to prove.
**Example (BANNED):** "Today I want to talk about why Itadori Yuji is actually the most tragic character in JJK."
**Why it's banned:** Nobody starts a real conversation like that. It sounds like a speech.

### 2. Evidence Sequence
**What it is:** A series of numbered or structured points presented in order to build a case.
**Example (BANNED):** "First, he loses everyone around him. Second, his grandfather's dying words haunt him. Third, Sukuna uses his body to kill people he cares about."
**Why it's banned:** It sounds like a listicle read aloud. Not a person talking.

### 3. Clean Conclusion
**What it is:** Wrapping the VO with a summary statement that ties back to the opener.
**Example (BANNED):** "So that's why Itadori is the most tragic character in JJK — every win comes at someone else's expense."
**Why it's banned:** This is how you close an essay. The Short already ended. Law #62 governs the actual closer format.

---

## WHAT CONVERSATIONAL LENGTH LOOKS LIKE

- 55–80 words lands naturally for most formats
- FACT_DROP and TRIVIA can go lower (40–60 words)
- CHARACTER_DIVE and ORIGIN_STORY can go higher (75–100 words) if the content earns it
- The test is not "how many words" — it is "does this sound like someone talking or someone writing"

---

## THE REAL TEST

Read the VO out loud. If it sounds like a presentation → rewrite. If it sounds like a person → pass.

## ENFORCEMENT

**Pre-send check (Law #58):** Before any VO is sent, it is reviewed against Law #59.
- Does it open with a thesis? → HARD STOP
- Does it have a numbered evidence sequence? → HARD STOP
- Does it close with a summary that mirrors the opener? → HARD STOP

If any banned structure is present → rewrite before send.
