# Law #81 — Platform Hashtag Limits (YouTube Shorts + TikTok)

**Status:** ACTIVE — June 28, 2026
**Source:** FluxNote YouTube Shorts Hashtag Strategy 2026 | PostWave TikTok Hashtag Algorithm 2026

---

## YouTube Shorts

**Hard limit:** 3 hashtags maximum in the title
**Optimal:** Exactly 3
**Mix:** 1 show/topic-specific tag + 1 content-angle tag + 1 channel tag (#AnimewithSebastian)
**Placement:** End of title, after the video title text
**Total title length:** Under 60 characters including hashtags
**Dead tags (never use):** #Shorts (YouTube identifies format automatically — wastes a slot), #Anime (too broad), #AnimeShorts (too broad), #Anime2026 (too broad/generic)
**Rule:** Niche-specific tags only. The more specific, the better.

---

## TikTok

**Hard cap:** None stated, but 10+ hashtags underperform by 22% in views
**Optimal:** 3–5 hashtags total
**Mix:**
- 1 niche show tag (e.g., #KillAo)
- 1–2 mid-range tags (100K–2M posts, e.g., #AnimeNews)
- 1 broad category tag (e.g., #Anime)
- Optional: #FYP (minor signal, not required)
**Caption:** Under 150 characters. TikTok reads caption text as heavily as hashtags.
**Never:** Recycle the same hashtag set across videos — TikTok deprioritizes repeated patterns.
**Never:** Use banned or restricted hashtags — even one suppresses the video.

---

## Enforcement
- Applies to ALL email packages: morning, evening, manual sends
- YouTube title block: EXACTLY 3 hashtags — no more, no less
- TikTok caption block: 3–5 hashtags — never over 5
- Cron must enforce at STEP 12 (hashtags) before send
- Missing or over-limit hashtag count = flag and correct before sending
