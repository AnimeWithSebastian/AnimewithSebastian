# Law #86 — VO Must Be Worth Saying

**Status:** ACTIVE
**Added:** June 28, 2026
**Updated:** June 29, 2026 — STRUCTURAL RULE added after live VO audit revealed both packages talking to warm up instead of making the point
**Applies to:** Every VO — both runtime slots, every manual package

## The Three Questions

Every VO must pass all three before it is sent:

1. What does this give the viewer they did not already have?
2. Could they get the same thing by just watching the show?
3. Is every sentence earning its place?

If it cannot pass all three = do not send.

## BANNED Patterns

- **Lecturing** — frontloading context before the point. Setting up what other people think before saying what you think.
- **Speaking to speak** — sentences with no logical dependency on each other. Each sentence must require the one before it.
- **Empty escalation** — ending that restates the hook or uses "that's a different kind of X" to signal the VO is ending without adding information.

## STRUCTURAL RULE (added June 29, 2026)

**The specific claim the video is making MUST be present in the first 2 lines. Not teased. Not set up. PRESENT.**

Everything after line 2 is evidence, texture, or escalation of that claim.

### What this means in practice

The VO has one job: make a claim the viewer didn't already have, then prove it.

Line 1–2 = the claim.
Line 3+ = the proof, the texture, the scene that makes it real.
Final line = the question the claim creates (Law #89 closer).

### Warmup lines are BANNED

All of the following are warmup patterns. They delay the point. Delaying the point = lecturing = FAIL:

- "Okay so —"
- "I'm not gonna lie —"
- "Everybody talks about X"
- "Most people think Y"
- "I went back and watched this and..."
- Listing what the audience already knows before making the point

### If the VO cannot state its point in the first 2 lines

The angle is not sharp enough. Do not try to fix the line — reframe the angle.

A weak angle produces a VO that warms up because it has nothing to say immediately.
A sharp angle produces a VO where the first line IS the point.

**Example of a sharp angle:**
"Solo Leveling sold you a power fantasy."
→ This is the point. It breaks the viewer's default assumption. It requires no warmup. Everything after it is proof.

**Example of a weak angle that produces warmup:**
"Solo Leveling is an interesting show because of how Jin-woo develops..."
→ This angle has no claim yet. The VO will warm up for 40 words before it gets to something.

### The VO must deliver one line the viewer can repeat to someone else

If no single line in the VO is quotable — specific, surprising, or perfectly phrased — the VO is not worth saying.

## Enforcement

Checked at STEP 10 as part of Core Checks (checks 1–4) and Hook Tension Check (check 5).
A VO that fails Law #86 does not get sent regardless of other scores.
