**Stage 1 — YPP Eligibility (active goal):**
- Need: 1,000 subscribers + 10M Shorts views in 90 days
- Current: ~69K total views, sub count in early growth
- Path: volume + conversion together. 2 videos/day (morning + evening) is the engine.

**Stage 2 — Direct Brand Outreach (can start NOW, before YPP):**
- Channel demographics (52.6% male 18–34) qualify for outreach to: Crunchyroll, Right Stuf Anime, manga publishers, anime figure/merch brands, VPNs, gaming peripherals
- Channels in the 1K–10K sub range close deals routinely when demographics are strong and niche is tight
- The media kit needs: audience demographics, top video performance, retention data, content identity statement

**Stage 3 — YPP Ad Revenue (post-1K subs):**
- Anime niche CPM: $2–4/1K views (lower than finance/tech but scales with volume)
- At 100K views/month = $200–400/month AdSense alone
- Brand deals will always outperform AdSense at this scale

## Rule

Every video sent must reinforce the media kit:
- **Consistent niche signal:** anime analysis, character depth, seasonal coverage
- **Consistent voice:** Anime with Sebastian — the channel has a POV
- **Consistent audience:** content that keeps pulling 18–34 male anime fans

## Media Kit Breakers (BANNED without strong justification)
- Off-niche videos that signal channel confusion to brands
- Content that looks like a different channel than the last 10 videos
- Formats that get views but contribute 0 subs (pure virality plays with no identity)

## Enforcement

Before sending any video package, ask: if a Crunchyroll brand manager saw this video alongside the last 5, would they immediately understand what this channel is and who it reaches?
If NO = reconsider the angle or format before sending.
