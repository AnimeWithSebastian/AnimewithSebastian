# Law #93 — Brand-Deal Demographic Lock

**Added:** June 29, 2026
**Source:** Channel analytics — YouTube Analytics API pull June 29 2026
**Applies to:** Every video recommendation

## Verified Channel Demographics (June 29, 2026)

| Segment | % of audience |
|---|---|
| Male 25–34 | 35.1% |
| Male 13–17 | 21.3% |
| Male 18–24 | 17.5% |
| Male 35–44 | 12.8% |
| **Combined male 18–34** | **52.6%** |

This is the demographic anime brands, gaming companies, VPNs, streaming services, and merch companies pay a premium to reach.

## Rule

Every video recommendation must be evaluated for its brand-deal demographic signal:

**HIGH brand value** — content that reinforces male 18–34 anime fan identity:
- Season previews and premiere guides
- Character analysis with genuine debate ("who actually won")
- Show comparisons and power rankings
- Hidden gem recommendations
- Rewatch-worthy moments with analysis

**MED brand value** — content that reaches but doesn't reinforce:
- Viral/trending clips without analysis layer
- Pure meme or reaction content

**LOW brand value** — content that dilutes the signal:
- Completely off-niche content
- Content aimed exclusively at 13–17 without 18–34 crossover
- Generic "top 10" lists with no original angle

## Requirement

At least 1 of every 3 videos must be explicitly HIGH brand-value content.
This is the media kit. Every video sent is evidence for the brand pitch.
