# Law #87 — Hook First Line Tension

**Added:** June 29, 2026
**Updated:** June 29, 2026 — APPROVED openers list purged. Old openers ("okay so", "I'm not gonna lie", "hear me out") reclassified as BANNED after live audit showed they delay the point and produce lecturing VOs.
**Source:** viralhookanalyzer.com — June 8, 2026 (5,000 Shorts study)
**Enforced in:** STEP 10 VO Quality Check (Hook Tension Check — Check #5)

## Rule

The first line of every VO must:
1. **Break the viewer's default assumption about the show or character**
2. **State OR strongly imply the point** — not tease it, not set up for it

The point IS the tension. If the hook creates tension without making the point, it is a warmup. Warmups are BANNED.

**Research basis:**
Viral Shorts hit first hard cut at a median of 0.9 seconds.
Control (non-viral) Shorts hit first hard cut at 2.4 seconds.
The 1.1-second window is the hard threshold — if the viewer is not pulled in before that cut, algorithm stops distributing.
A hook that opens with unresolved tension forces the viewer to watch the next line to resolve it.
A hook that opens with a warmup phrase signals "setup coming" — viewer leaves before the point arrives.

## APPROVED Hook Structure

**Statement that breaks the viewer's default assumption — point present in the break.**

The hook must do one of these:
- Reframe what the viewer thinks the show is actually about
- Contradict the common read of a character
- Make a specific claim that forces disagreement or curiosity

**PASS examples:**
- "Solo Leveling tricked you." → breaks assumption, implies a reframe is coming
- "Rimuru doesn't win because he's the strongest." → directly contradicts the common read
- "Jin-woo didn't escape the dungeon. The System just built him a nicer cage." → full reframe in one line

## BANNED Hook Openers (ALL of these are warmups)

Every one of these delays the point. None of them are the point. All BANNED:

- "okay so..."
- "hear me out..."
- "I'm not gonna lie..."
- "I went back and watched this..."
- "everybody talks about..."
- "most people think..."
- "you know what's crazy..."
- Any opener that implies *I have something to show you* before showing it
- Listing what the audience already knows as the opening move
- Starting with a question before making any claim (question closer = Law #89, question opener = warmup)

## Banned from Hook (Existing Rule — Unchanged)

- Starting with a verdict that closes the loop ("X is the best character because...")
- Any hook the viewer can mentally close before the second line

## Relationship to Law #86

Law #86 requires the point in the first 2 lines.
Law #87 requires the first line to break the viewer's assumption.
These work together: Law #87 ensures the hook creates the tension, Law #86 ensures the point is present before the viewer can leave.

If the first line passes Law #87 but the point is not present by line 2 = Law #86 fail.
If the point is present by line 2 but the first line is a warmup = Law #87 fail.
Both must pass. A VO cannot be sent on a Law #87 fail.

## Enforcement

Every VO must pass Hook Tension Check as Check #5 in STEP 10.
If hook opens with a warmup or conclusion = REVISE before sending.
A VO that fails Law #87 cannot pass STEP 10. It cannot be sent.
