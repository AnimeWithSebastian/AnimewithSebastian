# FORMAT REFERENCE — Seasonal/Recurring `format_type` Values

**Status:** REFERENCE NOTE (not a hard law). Added July 24, 2026, ported forward from
the retired word-count band in **Law #96** (see that file's RETIRED status header).

**Purpose:** `format_type` is a free-text field on each package with no fixed approved
list. This note documents four proven format concepts as usable `format_type` values
going forward. They are governed entirely by **CURRENT rules** — VO length/CTA/loop —
not by Law #96's original numbers:

- **VO length:** Law #138 — derives from the fixed CapCut edit duration. Default edit
  is 30 seconds → 100–108 words, target ~104 (not Law #96's original 60–90w band).
- **CTA:** current CTA law (Law #139) — specific question immediately followed by the
  exact phrase "Leave your take." (Law #62's question-closer rule is narrowed for this
  exact structure — see `laws/law_62_cut7_closer.md` SUPERSESSION NOTE.)
- **Loop:** Law #141/#147 seamless colon-handoff loop mechanics.
- **Fan voice:** Law #92 fan-voice standard still applies to all four.

Everything below is the four content concepts themselves — the angle requirements and
blackout rules — carried forward unchanged from Law #96. Only the word-count numbers
in Law #96's original text are retired; angle rules and blackout windows are NOT
retired and still apply as written here.

---

## SEASON_RATING
**What it is:** One show per video. A short, opinionated, provocative rating/verdict on
the current season's show — not a tier-list essay.

**Rules:**
- One show per Short. Never rank multiple shows in one video.
- State the rating in the hook (first 3 seconds) — do not build up to it.
- Rating must be provocative enough to be wrong; safe ratings get no comments.
- Must be a show currently airing OR finished within the last 30 days. No throwbacks.

**Blackout:** 7-day blackout per show. One rating per show per season — do not re-rate
the same show in the same season.

---

## SEASON_PREVIEW
**What it is:** Why the viewer should watch or look forward to a new anime, new season,
or new episode drop.

**Rules:**
- Requires a verified premiere date within 45 days (before or after airing). Never
  preview a show without a confirmed date.
- Angle must answer "why THIS show, for THIS viewer, right NOW" — not a trailer recap.
  Everyone has seen the trailer.
- Competitor check required: if the exact angle is already saturated, pivot the angle.

**Blackout:** 7-day blackout from send date, on top of the 45-day premiere window rule.

---

## MANGA_VS_ANIME
**What it is:** One specific, meaningfully different scene or moment between the manga
and anime versions — not a general "manga is better" take.

**Rules:**
- Must be a SPECIFIC scene/moment, not a general comparison — general takes have no
  search ceiling.
- The difference must be meaningful (a cut scene, a changed emotional beat, a panel
  that hits differently) — no surface-level "the colors are different" content.
- Must take a side. Neutral comparisons don't generate comments.
- Must verify the difference via a live source — never from memory (Law #52).

**Blackout:** 14-day blackout per show (longer shelf life — no same-show repeat within
two weeks).

---

## EPISODE_MOMENT
**What it is:** One single beat, scene, or reveal from a currently-airing show — one
moment, why it matters, what it means going forward.

**Rules:**
- Must air within 7 days of the episode dropping. After 7 days the search window
  closes — hold the package, do not publish stale.
- One moment per video, not a multi-beat recap. Pick the single biggest moment.
- Spoiler warning must be in the title or first line of the caption.
- Must be from a show with an active airing schedule — no reactions to episodes 2+
  seasons old.

**Blackout:** No blackout — episode-specific content is time-stamped and doesn't
compete with itself. The 7-day airing window is itself a hard deadline.

---

## What these are not (unchanged from Law #96)
- Not recap videos — the fan already watched it. The angle is always "here's what you
  missed / what it means / what's wrong with the popular read / what to watch for."
- Not essay-voice — must pass the Law #92 fan-voice read-aloud test.
- Not general opinions — "this season is mid" is not a SEASON_RATING; a specific,
  defended claim about one show is.
